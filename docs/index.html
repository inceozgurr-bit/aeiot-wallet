AEIOT Wallet

A self-custody wallet for iPhone. Your keys and your recovery phrase are created on your device and never leave it.

Common questions

I lost my phone. Can you recover my wallet?
No — and nobody else can either. That is what self-custody means. Your wallet lives in your 12-word recovery phrase: enter it in a new install and everything comes back. Without it, the coins are unreachable, permanently.
Where is my recovery phrase stored?
In the iPhone's Keychain, on that device only, behind Face ID or Touch ID. It is never uploaded, and the app has no server to upload it to.
I re-enrolled Face ID and the wallet will not open.
That is deliberate. Changing the enrolled face or fingerprint invalidates access to the stored phrase, so that someone who knows your passcode cannot add their own face and read it. Restore with your 12 words.
Which networks does it support?
Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Chain, Solana, the XRP Ledger and Bitcoin. The six EVM networks share one address; Solana, XRP and Bitcoin each have their own.
What is AEIOT?
A fixed-supply ERC-20 token on Base, total supply 1,150,115, with no way to create more. It carries no promise of value or return.
A transfer has not arrived.
Check the transaction on a block explorer using the link in the app. Transfers cannot be cancelled or reversed once sent, including transfers to a wrong address.
